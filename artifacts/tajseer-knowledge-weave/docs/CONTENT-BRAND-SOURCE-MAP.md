## Media inventory conclusions

- No verified customer photographs, case-study imagery, client video, local audio, authored learning screenshots or approved 3D service assets are present in `reference/` or the runtime Tajseer asset directory.
- The archived home copy references a hero MP4 and a YouTube video, but neither is supplied locally with reviewed rights, transcript or accessibility treatment.
- The ten supplied service PNGs are language-specific title graphics. They support service naming and the historic deep blue, but do not prove delivered work.
- All new dynamic diagrams are labeled as illustrative. No client, project, result, partnership, award, certification, team profile or measured outcome is implied.
