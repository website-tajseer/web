# Tajseer Hero storyboard — Digital Knowledge Canvas

## Art-direction statement

Tajseer’s Hero is a living editorial canvas built from its verified educational and service content. The visitor first sees one complete, premium composition: Tajseer’s identity, institutional position and a dense but controlled field of knowledge. Scrolling never replaces that composition. It curates it. Source material is cropped and brought into focus; relationships establish hierarchy; the same material becomes a structured learning system; structure becomes an applicable experience; and complexity resolves into a concise institutional outcome. Typography, content edges and the Tajseer blue binding persist between every frame. The final canvas remains visible while its baseline and binding extend into the company profile and source-supported 2009 history. Technology will later support the recomposition, focus and spatial hierarchy, but the concept works first as six static posters.

## Source / visual inventory

The `reference/` directory contains 22 Markdown/TXT files and no local photography, illustration, screenshot, audio, video, 3D or interface media. It provides the complete bilingual company profile, five service descriptions, mission, vision, contact details, source URLs and media references.

Real local visual assets are in `public/assets/tajseer/`:

- Original English SVG logo: `TajsserLogo.bc8c31e3b431f4abdefcf79540d093dc.svg`.
- Original Arabic raster logo: `arabic-logo.png`, 204×72.
- Ten Arabic/English service title images: `Services-01` through `Services-10`.
- Favicon.

The service images are archived title graphics, not project evidence. Their wave, network, globe, hexagon and brain/circuit illustrations are excluded from the Hero because several repeat explicitly rejected visual clichés. The storyboard uses the original English logo and verified text as its real content.

## Frame behavior

### Frame 01 — Arrival

- **Remains:** first frame; establishes the original logo, company position and unified knowledge canvas.
- **Transforms:** nothing yet.
- **Enters:** verified service terms and excerpts inside one dominant editorial composition.
- **Exits:** nothing.
- **Visitor understands:** Tajseer develops educational content and interactive experiences across five connected capabilities.
- **Real source assets:** English SVG logo; English About and five service source text; Thinking × Technology × Education from `media.md`.

### Frame 02 — Knowledge activates

- **Remains:** logo, headline, blue canvas, source excerpts and main typographic axis.
- **Transforms:** selected e-learning content is magnified; secondary content is cropped; relationships and focus become visible.
- **Enters:** focus boundary, editorial annotation and stronger hierarchy.
- **Exits:** no meaningful content; background excerpts recede but remain traceable.
- **Visitor understands:** information is being curated rather than replaced.
- **Real source assets:** the same logo and Multimedia/E-learning source text.

### Frame 03 — Knowledge becomes structured

- **Remains:** the same five service contents and blue/cyan editorial rules.
- **Transforms:** fragments align to a publication grid and connect to a purposeful learning center.
- **Enters:** hierarchy, sequencing and named capability relationships.
- **Exits:** the scattered arrival layout.
- **Visitor understands:** Tajseer turns related material into a coherent learning system.
- **Real source assets:** all five verified service names and their source-supported purposes.

### Frame 04 — Development / Application

- **Remains:** the content grid, service language and persistent blue canvas.
- **Transforms:** hierarchy becomes an applied sequence: content, design, interaction and delivery.
- **Enters:** source-supported SCORM, device and LMS/CMS/MOOCs delivery terms.
- **Exits:** abstract relationship lines after they have become a usable structure.
- **Visitor understands:** structured knowledge can now function as a digital learning experience.
- **Real source assets:** E-learning and Multimedia descriptions; no interface or project screenshot is implied.

### Frame 05 — Impact resolution

- **Remains:** Tajseer blue field, content baseline, educational purpose and brand binding.
- **Transforms:** complex content compresses into one confident editorial result.
- **Enters:** Thinking × Technology × Education and the source-supported company activity statement.
- **Exits:** intermediate modules and explanatory detail.
- **Visitor understands:** Tajseer connects content, technology and development into a coherent educational result.
- **Real source assets:** `media.md`, English About profile and original SVG logo.

### Frame 06 — Handoff to About

- **Remains:** 43% of the resolved Hero canvas, its blue binding, typographic axis and brand colors.
- **Transforms:** the binding becomes the About baseline; the vertical edge becomes the history axis.
- **Enters:** source-supported Since 2009 and the verified company profile before the Hero falls below meaningful occupancy.
- **Exits:** the remaining Hero canvas only after About is already readable.
- **Visitor understands:** the brand proposition now resolves into who Tajseer is and when it began.
- **Real source assets:** English SVG logo and English About source text.

## Transition occupancy map

| Transition interval | Hero meaningful occupancy | Incoming About occupancy | Meaningful combined occupancy |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Frame 05 settled | 88% | 0% | 88% |
| Handoff begins | 72% | 18% | 90% |
| Shared composition | 56% | 36% | 92% |
| Frame 06 | 43% | 51% | 94% |
| About takes over | 34% | 66% | 100% |

The outgoing Hero never falls below 40% before the 2009 history and company profile are meaningfully visible. The intended motion rule is **Persist → Transform → Recompose → Handoff**. Opacity is secondary to crop, position, scale, focus and shared alignment.
