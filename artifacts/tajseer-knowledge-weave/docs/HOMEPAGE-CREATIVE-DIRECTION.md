# Tajseer homepage — creative direction pass

## Selected narrative

**Tajseer bridges knowledge into impact.** The page moves from information, to learning, to applied capability. Each section has its own tempo and visual metaphor, while the teal, warm ivory, deep blue, editorial type and fine-line system keep the experience coherent.

## Hero — knowledge gains direction

1. **Core message:** Tajseer structures knowledge until it can create impact.
2. **Feeling:** assured, intelligent, established and forward-looking.
3. **Visual concept:** several authored information streams weave into one directed spatial field.
4. **Composition:** dominant HTML statement on one side; an off-axis spatial weave extends beyond the opposite edge with foreground, middle-ground and background layers.
5. **Typography:** the headline anchors the composition; small phase labels sit inside the knowledge system instead of floating as interface chrome.
6. **3D / motion:** WebGL gives the weave real occlusion, light response and depth. It is the only homepage WebGL scene.
7. **Scroll:** streams move from separated knowledge layers toward alignment and impact; the composition opens before About.
8. **Interaction:** scrolling changes the active stage from Knowledge to Learning, Development, Application and Impact.
9. **Transition:** the spatial field thins into the curved editorial line used by Since 2009.
10. **Why Tajseer:** the weave expresses the company's role as a connector between knowledge, designed learning and practical outcomes without drawing a literal bridge.

## About — time and credibility

1. **Core message:** Tajseer has worked from Saudi Arabia since 2009.
2. **Feeling:** calm, credible and grounded.
3. **Visual concept:** time as editorial scale.
4. **Composition:** the year and company profile balance across generous space.
5. **Typography:** 2009 acts as the image; the profile stays readable and factual.
6. **3D / motion:** none; restrained reveal is enough.
7. **Scroll:** the year arrives before the profile.
8. **Interaction:** none required.
9. **Transition:** a fine arc carries the eye into the dark multimedia field.
10. **Why Tajseer:** it gives the verified founding date institutional weight without inventing milestones.

## Interactive Multimedia — media behaves like media

1. **Core message:** Tajseer creates and combines image, sound and motion.
2. **Feeling:** expressive, crafted and energetic.
3. **Visual concept:** crop, waveform and temporal displacement coexist in one composition.
4. **Composition:** editorial copy sits beside three oversized media words and an animated signal.
5. **Typography:** IMAGE, SOUND and MOTION become active visual material.
6. **3D / motion:** CSS/SVG motion gives each word different behavior; WebGL would add no useful meaning.
7. **Scroll:** image reveals, sound travels and motion changes position at different rates.
8. **Interaction:** pointer movement/hover adds a restrained second response.
9. **Transition:** the expressive signal resolves into the ruled structure of E-learning.
10. **Why Tajseer:** the section demonstrates the production disciplines named in the verified service copy.

## E-learning — information becomes a learning experience

1. **Core message:** Tajseer turns raw material into structured digital learning.
2. **Feeling:** ordered, methodical and dependable.
3. **Visual concept:** an editorial transformation sequence.
4. **Composition:** service statement and four progressively activated stages.
5. **Typography:** stage names are the main diagram.
6. **3D / motion:** 2.5D depth, scale and line growth communicate hierarchy clearly.
7. **Scroll:** one stage at a time gains weight, order and connection.
8. **Interaction:** hover focuses any stage without interrupting scroll.
9. **Transition:** the final digital-learning line becomes the measurement grid in STEM.
10. **Why Tajseer:** it makes the service process visible without claiming a proprietary workflow.

## STEM — force makes cause and effect visible

1. **Core message:** STEM learning becomes clear through experiment and consequence.
2. **Feeling:** curious, precise and hands-on.
3. **Visual concept:** a simply supported beam responds visibly to applied force.
4. **Composition:** the service message sits beside a large laboratory diagram and live readout.
5. **Typography:** QUESTION, EXPLORE, TEST and UNDERSTAND frame the experiment.
6. **3D / motion:** SVG is selected because exact, legible cause and effect matters more than rendered volume.
7. **Scroll:** the force enters, the beam bends and the measurement becomes visible.
8. **Interaction:** a range control changes the force and resulting deflection immediately.
9. **Transition:** the measurement grid darkens and becomes the media-coordinate field of AR/VR.
10. **Why Tajseer:** it demonstrates the verified STEM service through inquiry rather than a decorative science symbol.

## AR / VR — perception gains depth

1. **Core message:** Tajseer moves learning content from viewing toward spatial presence.
2. **Feeling:** immersive, perceptual and focused.
3. **Visual concept:** one editorial media spread separates into contextual spatial layers, then returns to the page.
4. **Composition:** copy remains an anchor while media layers move through foreground, middle-ground and background.
5. **Typography:** labels stay attached to their media surfaces and scale with them.
6. **3D / motion:** CSS 3D perspective supplies meaningful depth and occlusion while keeping the content crisp.
7. **Scroll:** flat → layered → spatial → flat.
8. **Interaction:** scroll controls depth directly; no game controls or free camera.
9. **Transition:** all planes collapse back to a single editorial baseline before Consulting.
10. **Why Tajseer:** the visitor feels the change in perception described by the verified AR/VR service.

## Training & Consulting — capability becomes institutional change

1. **Core message:** Tajseer develops people, capability and organizations through consultancy and training.
2. **Feeling:** mature, strategic and consequential.
3. **Visual concept:** a capability map grows from expertise to institutional transformation.
4. **Composition:** a large statement faces a rigorous strategic map with a central Tajseer axis.
5. **Typography:** the four verified themes carry the visual hierarchy.
6. **3D / motion:** editorial lines, scale and controlled sequencing fit the institutional tone.
7. **Scroll:** nodes connect progressively into one strategic direction.
8. **Interaction:** hover isolates each capability without creating dashboard behavior.
9. **Transition:** the final transformation line becomes the dividing rules in Mission / Vision.
10. **Why Tajseer:** it gives consulting equal weight and frames the company as a capability partner.

## Mission / Vision and Contact

Mission / Vision uses emotional clarity, large quoted language and a deep-blue field. Contact resolves the page in warm light with the verified Riyadh address, email and phone numbers. Neither section needs 3D or interactive ornament.

## Senior art-direction critique

Rejected concepts included a futuristic room, a central media wall, a tunnel, a literal bridge, an atom/orbit motif, a floating product object, a headset and a collection of unrelated service scenes. Each was either generic, too literal, repeated an earlier visual trick or began from an available technology instead of the business message.

The selected direction earns its techniques: WebGL is reserved for the signature spatial weave where real occlusion and light establish brand depth; SVG explains the STEM experiment precisely; CSS 3D changes perception in AR/VR while preserving typography; the remaining sections use editorial composition and motion.
